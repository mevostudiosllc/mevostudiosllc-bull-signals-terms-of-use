Fast Mover Notifications

The App may automatically send "Fast Mover" push notifications when a stock or cryptocurrency on your personal watchlist experiences significant price movement. These notifications are calculated from third-party price data and apply only to symbols you have added to your watchlist. Fast Mover notifications include:

  • Fast Daily Mover: Triggered when a symbol moves 10% or more up or down in a single trading day, with additional alerts at every 5% increment (15%, 20%, 25%, etc.).
  • Fast Weekly Mover: Triggered when a symbol moves 50% or more up or down over a rolling 7-day period, with additional alerts at 75%, 100%, and every 25% increment thereafter.
  • Fast Monthly Mover: Triggered when a symbol moves 50% or more up or down from the 1st of the current calendar month (checked after the 7th day of the month), with additional alerts at 75%, 100%, and every 25% increment thereafter.
  • Fast Yearly Mover: Triggered when a symbol moves 100% or more up or down from January 1st of the current calendar year, with additional alerts at 200% and every 100% increment thereafter.

Each threshold level is sent at most once per period per symbol to prevent duplicate notifications. If multiple time periods trigger simultaneously for the same symbol (e.g., weekly, monthly, and yearly all at once), the first notification is sent immediately and subsequent ones are spaced at least 30 minutes apart. These notifications will NOT be sent if you have disabled notifications in your device settings (Settings > Notifications > Bull Signals) or if you have disabled notifications within the App's menu. Both Apple system notifications and in-app notifications must be enabled to receive Fast Mover alerts.
